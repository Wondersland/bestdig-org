Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026. This policy explains how bestdig.org may collect, use and share information when you use the website.

Information we may collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address and message when you contact us or subscribe to communications.

When you visit the site, we and service providers may automatically receive technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address and interaction data.

How we use information

  • Operate, secure and improve the website
  • Respond to inquiries and correction requests
  • Understand site usage and content performance
  • Measure advertising and affiliate activity
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies, pixels, local storage and similar technologies may be used for essential site functions, analytics, advertising measurement, personalization and affiliate attribution. The available controls depend on your location and the tools deployed on the site.

Where required, non-essential technologies will be handled through a consent mechanism. You can also adjust browser settings, although blocking certain technologies may affect site functionality.

How information may be shared

Information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, analytics, security, email, form processing, advertising measurement and affiliate tracking. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction.

We do not sell personal information for money. Some advertising or analytics activities may be considered “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising under certain privacy laws.

Your privacy choices

Depending on your location and the technologies in use, you may be able to manage cookie preferences, opt out of certain targeted advertising, unsubscribe from emails or submit a privacy request.

Regional privacy rights

Residents of certain jurisdictions may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of personal information, and to object to or opt out of certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted by law. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, security and operational needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party links

Our pages may link to retailers, manufacturers and other third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Policy changes

We may update this policy to reflect legal, technical or business changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when material updates are made.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact: privacy@bestdig.org. Replace this address with your monitored privacy contact before publication.
